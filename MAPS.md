# Map downloads

The 25 stock maps come from [Counter-Strike on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/10/).
These 42 custom maps have independent public sources. Extract archive resources
into the matching `cstrike/` paths. Raw `.bsp` files go in `cstrike/maps/`.

Download links were found on the source pages on 31 October 2026. A listed
download is not a promise that its revision matches Slash. The maps marked
**matched** were downloaded and their BSP SHA256 hashes match our snapshot.
[All snapshot hashes and source pages](Rebuild/map-sources.json).

Keep all resources from map archives: textures (`.wad`), skies (`gfx/env`),
models, sprites and sounds can be required. Preserve filename case on Linux.
For missing/outdated bot navigation, host the map and run `bot_nav_analyze`.

Creator pages for [de_westwood](https://gamebanana.com/mods/85382) and
[fy_pool_day](https://gamebanana.com/mods/86562) are additional sources.
This catalog does not require a private Slash archive or our finished packages.

`breakfloor_b5` by Anssi Kivitie is included in this repository because the old
listing has no working download server. Its hash matches the packaged snapshot.
